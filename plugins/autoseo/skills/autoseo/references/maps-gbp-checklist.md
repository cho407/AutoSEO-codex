# Public business-profile checklist

Use only fields visible on the public profile, the official website, structured
data, or a user-provided export.

- verified business name;
- primary and secondary categories;
- storefront address or truthful service area;
- local phone and canonical website URL;
- regular and special hours;
- description, services, products, and attributes;
- appointment, order, reservation, or menu links where applicable;
- current photos and logo;
- public questions and owner answers;
- recent review themes and response patterns;
- accessibility and amenity facts;
- consistency with contact pages and local schema.

Mark unavailable fields as `not observed`. Never infer verification status,
ownership, hidden settings, or unpublished profile fields.
